- **Delegate inboxes, part two: `scoutctl inbox apply` and `inbox revert`** (#550 V4a2, spec §7.1a; `engine/scout/delegate_apply.py`). `inbox apply <id>|--all` applies the action-item proposals that `inbox pull` took, each at most once, in ULID order.
  - **What is applied.** Only a copy whose bytes match the HMAC in the signed record is applied (otherwise `not_taken`). The item must be one the delegate saw: a task with that `[#TAG]` in that date's daily file, in its signed `last_mirror` or `pending_mirror`. It must also still be visible now, with its whole block surviving the delegate's redaction. Otherwise the outcome is `unknown_item`, the same whether the item is hidden or missing. With no mirror at all, it is `no_mirror`, and the next apply asks again.
  - **The ops.** `mark-done`, `snooze`, `set-estimate`, `set-block` and `clear-block` are applied through the action-items commands, by id only. `add-comment` is `held` until the review queue (V4b): it would put the agent's text where sending runs read it. Observations wait for V4b too.
  - **Limits.** Each op has a daily cap per delegate, and at most 5 of one op are applied per apply; the rest are `limited`. A snooze must be within 60 days. A proposal is `stale` after a week. An op a crash interrupted is recorded as `interrupted` and is never applied again.
  - **Undo.** Each applied op records its task's lines before and after, in the signed record. `scoutctl inbox revert <ULID>` (the owner only) puts them back, unless the task has changed since (`revert_conflict`).
  - **What apply doesn't do.** Outcomes are recorded by ULID, never with the proposal's text. Nothing is committed: the runner will commit (V4b).
