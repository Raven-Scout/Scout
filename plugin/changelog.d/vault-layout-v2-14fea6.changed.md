- **The vault has a new folder layout, layout v2** (ADR 0011; `engine/scout/layout.py`, `engine/scout/layout_migrate.py`). Each kind of file has one place: Scout's system under `scout/` (`scout/brain/` for the brain skills, `scout/runners/`, `scout/scripts/`, `scout/hooks/`, `scout/kb-page-standard.md`), Scout's records of its own runs under `scout/records/` (session log, mistake audit, connector health, dreaming proposals, wishlist), generated content in `action-items/`, `drafts/` and a new `reports/`, knowledge in `knowledge-base/`, and user input in `inbox/` (`inbox/inbox.md`, `inbox/drop/`). `scout-config.yaml` and the dot-folders stay where they are, and a generated root `README.md` maps the folders. Setup creates layout v2; every upgrade moves an existing vault (see the upgrade notes).
- **Spaces.** `knowledge-base/<space>/` holds a space's projects, topics, sources, meetings and role notes; `spaces:` in `scout-config.yaml` lists them (`work` is the default, `personal` is private). Each space has a default visibility, so a private space's notes are private without a line in each. People and organizations get a Scout-wide identity note (`knowledge-base/people/<name>.md`, `knowledge-base/organizations/<name>.md`) and a role note per space; the knowledge graph folds the role notes into their identity and takes the strictest privacy label of the files it read, and kb lint refuses facts written into an identity note.
- **Meetings have one home.** The root `meetings/` and `knowledge-base/meetings/` merge into `knowledge-base/<space>/meetings/<slug>/`, and the two registries into `knowledge-base/meetings.md`, one row per series with its space. The other cells of each row move to the series' home file, and text that isn't a series row (notes, event logs, retired series) is kept as written under "Kept from the old registries". Dated occurrence files are `type: meeting-occurrence`, with their own kb lint and freshness class.
- **`scoutctl vault`**: `layout --json` (the engine's path table, a contract with the app), `migrate --plan` / `--apply`, `space-candidates` and `move <path> --space <key>` for the space review after the move.
