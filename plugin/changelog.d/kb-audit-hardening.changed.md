- **`kb structure` holds "Where it stands" to the page standard** (`engine/scout/kb/structure.py`): a section with more than `max_bullets` entries (default 6) or an entry over `max_bullet_chars` (300) now fails the check, and `kb lint`'s `page-bullet` also refuses a commit that grows the section past the limit. Before, a page passed however many bullets it had, and however long they were.
- **Staleness counts what lands outside the project folder**: a newer commit to a source or topic note (`kb_structure.linked_dirs`) that links to the project, by name, alias or a path into its folder, now makes the front page stale. A private linking note counts without its date being shown.
- **The nightly KB pass picks by priority × staleness** (days since `as_of`; 🔴 4, 🟡 3, 🟢 2, none 1) and never starts a full rebuild (sections missing, or over budget) of a front page over `nightly_max_bytes` (12 KB): `kb structure` marks it `[interactive]`, `--next` names it on stderr with how long it has waited, and `scoutctl doctor` notes the pages waiting. A page that only needs a refresh is still picked whatever its size, a rebuild or shrink already under way finishes, and a shared page it may only trim is still picked. An `as_of` that can't be read is reported as `unreadable as_of`. Before, one big page could take night after night, and small pages with no priority took nights from projects.
- **The dreaming fact-check pass is a driver output and a pre-commit check** (`engine/scout/kb/fact_check.py`), modelled on `action-items check-demotion`: `pre-session data` writes `fact_check_targets` (KB notes changed in the last 3 days and not re-verified since, most consequential first, labelled), and `scoutctl kb check-fact-check` exits 1 until 2 of them (all, if fewer) carry today's `last_verified:` and a `verified_against:`. Shared notes are never targets, and a target moved or archived since counts as done. `--skipped "<reason>"` accepts a run with no live source and prints the line for its session-log row. An accepted skip is recorded as a `kb.fact_check_skipped` event, `scoutctl doctor` notes the pass skipped 3 or more times in a week, and a `last_verified:` dated in the future doesn't count. Targets are computed for dreaming runs only. Left to prose, most dreaming runs skipped it.
