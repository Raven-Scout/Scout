- **`kb structure` holds "Where it stands" to the page standard** (`engine/scout/kb/structure.py`): a section with more than `max_bullets` entries (default 6) or an entry over `max_bullet_chars` (300) now fails the check, and `kb lint`'s `page-bullet` also refuses a commit that grows the section past the limit. Before, a page passed with 12 bullets, or with 5 of its 6 over 300 characters.
- **Staleness counts what lands outside the project folder**: a newer commit to a source or topic note (`kb_structure.linked_dirs`) that links to the project, by name, alias or a path into its folder, now makes the front page stale. A private linking note counts without its date being shown.
- **The nightly KB pass picks by priority × staleness** (days since `as_of`; 🔴 4, 🟡 3, 🟢 2, none 1) and never rebuilds a front page over `nightly_max_bytes` (12 KB): `kb structure` marks it `[interactive]` and `--next` names it on stderr for the run's report. A shared page it may only trim is still picked. Before, five nights went to one ~13 KB page and two to small pages with no priority.
- **The dreaming fact-check pass is a driver output and a pre-commit check** (`engine/scout/kb/fact_check.py`), modelled on `action-items check-demotion`: `pre-session data` writes `fact_check_targets` (KB notes changed in the last 3 days and not re-verified since, most consequential first, labelled), and `scoutctl kb check-fact-check` exits 1 until 2 of them (all, if fewer) carry today's `last_verified:` and a `verified_against:`. A `fact-check skipped: <reason>` on today's session-log row accepts a run with no live source. Left to prose, the pass ran in 3 of 50 sessions.
