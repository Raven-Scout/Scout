- **One pill, one filter chip and one tag** (`Scout/Utilities/DSChip.swift`; design-system v2). Status pills (proposals, wishlist items and their priority, session states, on-miss policy, updates, run protection, Health risk, the menu-bar urgent count) are `DSPill`: a capsule wash in the tone's colour with a hairline, mixed-case text in the tone's ink. "Pending" and "in progress" use a new readable blue instead of the slot-type blue, which was too faint to read. Filter chips on Action Items, Schedules, Sessions and the topic board look and behave the same: raised paper with a hairline, inverted when selected (Action Items used a pressed look, the topic board an outline), with hover, pressed, disabled and keyboard-focus states. Source, link and data tags on cards are one boxed `.dsTag`. The Knowledge Base editor's Save is the primary button, and the topic-board legend now shows keyboard focus.
- **One banner** (`Scout/Utilities/DSBanner.swift`). On battery, connector alerts, run health, Schedules' "changed outside the app" and "unsaved edits were dropped", the KB editor's "changed on disk", a run's protected-file notice and the diagnostics review notice share one look: a rounded wash of the tone (info, warning, error), its symbol, the text in the regular inks, the first action as the primary button, and a Dismiss ×. A critical connector alert is now the error banner with a Details button, not a solid red bar, and the Control Center's banners stack with even spacing.
- **Cards draw their own states** (`editorialCard` in `Scout/Utilities/DesignSystem.swift`). Task, board, session, schedule (list and timeline), wishlist, Preamble, topic, Settings and menu-bar cards use the one card. Selected is the same accent outline everywhere (it was a wash, an ink outline, a type-coloured ring or a leading bar, depending on the page), clickable cards darken their hairline on hover and show keyboard focus, and the coloured left edge is one 3 pt bar. The timeline's slot cards lose their drop shadow.
- Guard tests (`ChipStyleGuardTests`, `BannerStyleGuardTests`, `CardStyleGuardTests`) fail on hand-built pills, chip boxes, status washes, banners and card chrome, and the contrast test now checks every pill tone and banner tone in light, dark and both Increase Contrast modes.
