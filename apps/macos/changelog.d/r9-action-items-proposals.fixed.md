- **Topics: a legend filter no longer pins every topic open** (`Scout/ActionItems/TopicBoard/TopicBoardView.swift`, `BoardExpansion.swift`). Topics still open when you pick a state, but their chevrons work again: a topic you close stays closed until the filter changes. The × on an Inbox or Loose ends entry is always drawn, faint until hovered, and its tooltip says Undo brings the entry back.
- **Settings ▸ Action Items ▸ Done range now changes the Topics board** (`TopicBoardView.swift`, `ActionItemsView.swift`). The board kept its own copy of the range, so the setting did nothing. Both now read and write `actionItems.topics.doneRange` in `scout-display.json`; clicking the range above the Done list changes the setting too. A range picked on the board before this update is not carried over.
- **An older day's Topics board shows that day as it stood** (`TopicBoard.referenceDate`). Snoozes and the Done list's "today" and "yesterday" were judged against the real today, so a task snoozed to the next day looked active. They are now judged against the end of the displayed day.
- **Snooze presets count from the real today** (`Scout/ActionItems/Views/SnoozePopoverView.swift`, `SnoozeDates`). On an older day's file, "Tomorrow" counted from that file's day was already in the past.
- **Proposals with a status Scout doesn't recognise stay with the undecided ones** (`Scout/Proposals/Models/ProposalStatus.swift`). They were filed under Resolved, out of sight. They now count toward the sidebar badge, say that Scout doesn't recognise the status, and get Approve and Decline.
