- **A Changes tab (⌘6) shows what Scout changed about itself, and lets you undo it.** Each change Scout makes to its own instructions or scripts gets a card: what it changed, why in plain words, how its test is going, and the diff. Undo asks first, then queues the undo for Scout's next run, or says why Scout can't undo it. Mark wrong flags a change that hurt. A strip at the top holds the changes to what Scout may do on its own; they wait there until you approve or reject them after reading the diff. A chart shows how much you've used Scout over time, with each change marked on it.
  - On first visit the tab asks once whether Scout may count your Claude sessions outside the vault that used Scout (only that one did, never what it read). You can change the answer in Settings.
  - An edit you make in the app (action items, boards, knowledge-base files) now counts as a sign you rely on Scout.
