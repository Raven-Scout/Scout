- A new version of Scout no longer goes unnoticed for up to a day. Scout now checks for updates every hour instead of once a day (`Scout-Info.plist`). When a scheduled check finds a new version, Scout shows a "Scout X is ready" banner at the top of every page, with **Update Now**, and sends one macOS notification for that version. Sparkle's own update window no longer opens by itself, where it could land behind other apps; the banner and the notification bring it up (`Scout/Shell/AppUpdateBanner.swift`, gentle reminders in `Scout/Services/Updates/AppUpdater.swift`).
