- A new version of Scout no longer goes unnoticed for up to a day. Scout now checks for updates every hour instead of once a day (`Scout-Info.plist`); while an update it found waits for you, it doesn't check again until you install, skip or close that one. When a scheduled check finds a new version, Scout shows a "Scout X is ready" banner at the top of every page, with **Update now**, and sends one macOS notification for that version (as soon as Scout's notifications are allowed, if they're off). Sparkle's own update window no longer opens by itself, where it could land behind other apps; the banner and the notification bring it up, and a click while the update is still downloading opens it once it's ready (`Scout/Shell/AppUpdateBanner.swift`, gentle reminders in `Scout/Services/Updates/AppUpdater.swift`).
