- **A banner above a note no longer throws the window's content up under the title bar.** Opening a note
  with an "Unsaved draft" banner, or getting the "changed on disk" banner, moved everything up by about
  245 pt or more: sidebar rows sat over the window buttons, and the note's header and the banner went out of
  reach until you opened a note without one. The window measures its smallest size at almost no width,
  where a banner's text wrapped a word per line and asked for 1,500–2,300 pt of height; the note column now
  never asks for more height than the window gives it.
- **The note header gives the path more room.** With the sidebar, file tree and Links panel open at
  1280–1512 pt, the folders in the path shrank to a clipped letter with no ellipsis, and the note's name was
  cut to about 8 characters before the controls moved to their own row. The folders now truncate to "p…s" at
  the least, and the controls move to their own row sooner: the path and name keep at least 280 pt beside
  them, not 160.
